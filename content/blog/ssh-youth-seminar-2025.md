Save and Serve Humanity organized a youth seminar on 6 August 2025 at the Renewal Center, Rohinala, Youhanabad, bringing young people together for an evening centered on faith, purpose, responsibility, and positive change.

The seminar was held under the theme **"بااختیار نوجوان، باوقار قوم"** - _Empowered Youth, Dignified Nation_ - with the message **"From Prayer to Power, This is YOUR Hour."** The theme challenged young people not only to pray for change, but also to recognize their own responsibility in becoming part of that change.

Throughout the seminar, participants were encouraged to think seriously about their identity, abilities, choices, and role within their communities. The message emphasized that empowerment begins when young people combine faith with discipline, knowledge, courage, and action. Prayer provides direction and strength, but that conviction must also be reflected in the way a person studies, serves, leads, works, and responds to the needs around them.

The words of 1 Timothy 4:12 capture this responsibility clearly: **"Let no one despise your youth."** Young people were reminded that their age does not prevent them from becoming examples in character, faith, service, and leadership.

Through initiatives such as this youth seminar, Save and Serve Humanity continues to expand its work beyond practical community support into the development and encouragement of the next generation. SSH believes that young people should not simply wait for the future to arrive. With the right guidance, opportunities, faith, and sense of responsibility, they can begin shaping their communities today.

The seminar's central message remained simple but powerful: **this is their hour - an hour to pray, prepare, act, serve, and become a generation capable of building a stronger and more dignified society.**
