In Laweray Village, Hafizabad, SSH supported church construction for a Christian community that had been gathering in a small and fragile worship space.

The project helped provide a safer and more dignified place for worship. It is part of SSH's wider history of faith-inspired community welfare and church-related service.

This story should be understood as a specific project record: a local act of service shaped by faith, practical need, and community life.
