During the COVID-19 lockdown period, SSH carried out ration distribution for families facing job loss, food insecurity, and uncertainty.

The support included basic food and household essentials such as flour, rice, lentils, cooking oil, sugar, tea, milk, baby food, sanitizers, and masks.

This work belongs in SSH's community welfare archive as a record of practical support during crisis. It reflects a simple but urgent kind of service: helping families meet immediate needs when normal routines had broken down.
