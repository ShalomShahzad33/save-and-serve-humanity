On 20 November 2025, Shahzad Masih, President of Save and Serve Humanity, attended the oath taking ceremony of Advocate Riaz Anjum, President of the Christian Lawyers Association Pakistan.

Advocate Riaz Anjum and the newly elected CLAP cabinet took oath at the ceremony. Ramesh Singh Arora, Provincial Minister for Minority Affairs, administered the oath.

The event was also attended by religious leaders including Senior Pastor Anwar Fazal, Dr. Majeed Abel, Bishop Azad Marshall, and Bishop Leo Paul. Their presence connected the gathering to wider themes of justice, unity, faith, and service.

SSH's presence at this event reflects community solidarity and support for institutions and leaders working toward justice and representation.
