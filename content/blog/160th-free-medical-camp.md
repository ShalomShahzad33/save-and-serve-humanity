Save and Serve Humanity, in collaboration with Chief Cornerstone Fellow Church Ministries, conducted SSH's 160th Free Medical Camp as a two-day health outreach for underserved communities.

More than 400 patients received free care during the camp. The outreach continued SSH's long-running medical camp history, providing practical support through checkups, medicines, and the service of doctors, nurses, and volunteers.

This milestone builds on SSH's wider record of numbered medical camps. Earlier archive records include the 147th free medical camp in 2022; this 2025 camp shows that the medical outreach continued beyond that earlier public milestone.

SSH's medical camp history is connected to a wider story of service that reaches back to 2005. That history should be handled with care: the 160th camp is a confirmed milestone for this specific record, while the current lifetime total may continue to grow as new camp records are added.
