On June 10, 2023, Save and Serve Humanity organized a free medical camp at a brick kiln in Kasur, focused on women and children.

The medical team included one lady doctor, one charge nurse, two assistant nurses, one dispenser, and three volunteers. Together, they provided care for 155 patients, along with nutritional supplements and hygiene kits.

The camp responded to difficult working conditions faced by brick-kiln families. Women and children in these communities can face heat exposure, dust, poor nutrition, and limited access to regular healthcare.

This record strengthens SSH's Kasur brick-kiln archive by adding a specific event date, staffing detail, services, and patient count for one documented outreach.
